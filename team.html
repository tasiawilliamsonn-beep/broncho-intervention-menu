Counselor Check-In Notes

For things you've noticed that may not need a PowerSchool log entry but should be on the counselor's and administrators' radar. Use initials only. Notes stay on this device until resolved, and open notes carry over to next week.

Don't wait for the meeting

  • Suspected abuse or neglect: Indiana law requires you to report it yourself, right away, to DCS at 1-800-800-5556 or to law enforcement, then tell the principal. Telling the counselor or principal does not replace your report (IC 31-33-5).
  • Talk of suicide or self-harm, or a threat to hurt someone: stay with the student and get the counselor or an administrator now. Follow Sunnyside's crisis procedures. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available by call or text.
  • Weapons, drugs, or immediate danger: Level 4. Contact an administrator immediately.

Calibration Activity

Use this during a team meeting, about once a month, so every Sunnyside staff member responds to behavior the same way.

Agenda: 30 minutes

  1. Why it matters (5 min)
  2. Scenarios (20 min): decide silently, vote, reveal, talk.
  3. Team agreements (5 min): edit, print, sign.

Why it matters

  • Discipline gaps are biggest when behavior is labeled with subjective words like "defiance."
  • When responses differ by classroom, students get different chances to learn.
  • Shared definitions and a shared process make discipline fair.

Smolkowski et al., 2016; Girvan et al., 2017; Liu, Penner, & Gao, 2023; McIntosh et al., 2018
