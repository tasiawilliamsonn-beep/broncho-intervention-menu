Team Meeting

A short activity to make sure every Sunnyside staff member responds to behavior the same way.

Agenda: 30 minutes

  1. Why it matters (5 min)
  2. Scenarios (20 min): decide silently, vote, reveal, talk.
  3. Team agreements (5 min): edit, print, sign.

Why it matters

  • Discipline gaps are biggest when behavior is labeled with subjective words like "defiance."
  • When responses differ by classroom, students get different chances to learn.
  • Shared definitions and a shared process make discipline fair.

Smolkowski et al., 2016; Girvan et al., 2017; Liu, Penner, & Gao, 2023; McIntosh et al., 2018
