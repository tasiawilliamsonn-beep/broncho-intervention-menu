The Research Behind the Menu

Every intervention on this site is tied to peer-reviewed research or to the professional texts cited in our MTSS Tier 1 training. Nothing on the menu is included because it is popular or easy; it is included because it has evidence.

How interventions were chosen

Each strategy appears in at least one peer-reviewed study or review (for example, the Journal of Applied Behavior Analysis, Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions, School Psychology Review) or in the research-cited Tier 1 toolkit presented in our ONC BOCES training.
