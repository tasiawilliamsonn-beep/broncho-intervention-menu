Parent Contact

Families are our partners. Use these tips and scripts to make every call home clear, respectful, and focused on helping the student.

Before you call

  • Make a positive contact early, before there is ever a problem.
  • Have one specific, objective example ready.
  • Know the Tier 1 intervention you are using.
  • Check the family's preferred language and contact.
  • Call when you are calm and have 10 minutes.

During the call

  • Start with a genuine strength.
  • Describe what you saw and heard, not labels.
  • Share what you're doing to help.
  • Ask what works at home, and listen.
  • Agree on a plan and a check-back date.

After the call

  • Log it in PowerSchool as Communication.
  • Follow up within a week, especially with good news.
  • Share concerns with your team and counselor.
  • For Level 3 and 4, an administrator contacts the family. Coordinate first.
