Behavior happens for a reason. Describe the Antecedent (what is happening right before), the Behavior, and the Consequence (what usually happens right after). The most frequent outcome is often the pay-off that keeps the behavior going (Packenham, Shute, & Reid, 2004).

Tier 1 Classroom Self-Check

Well-managed classrooms rest on five foundations: taught expectations, motivating instruction, group management, relationships, and flexible individual management. Rate your classroom honestly. Before deciding a student needs Tier 2, make sure these Tier 1 practices are in place. Your ratings stay on this device.

Daily Behavior Report

A Daily Behavior Report is a quick, reliable way to show whether an intervention is working (Chafouleas, Riley-Tillman, & Sugai, 2007). Rate the same behaviors at the same time each day for 2 to 4 weeks. To keep ratings consistent, decide in advance what a typical classmate would earn (for example, 7 = matches the class norm).

Behavior Checklist Maker

Positive behaviors must be taught. Break a goal behavior into small, observable steps (a task analysis), then teach it in four stages: show them, watch and praise them, practice, and prompt it across settings (Kazdin, 2013). Students can use the printed card to self-monitor (Bruhn, McDaniel, & Kreigh, 2015).
