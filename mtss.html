Sunnyside MTSS Team

The team that helps when a student needs more than Tier 1 support. Start with documented Tier 1 interventions, then bring your data to the team.

Who is on the team

How to bring a student to the team

  1. Use Tier 1 first. Choose matched interventions from the Intervention Menu and log each one in the Log Builder.
  2. Contact the family. Log each contact as Communication. See Parent Contact.
  3. Give each intervention 2 to 3 weeks. Track it with the Daily Behavior Report. Use at least 3 interventions before referring (about 6 to 9 weeks).
  4. Print the Request for Support packet from the Proof of Intervention record in the Log Builder.
  5. Submit it to the team using the process on the right.

When and how we meet

    The Tier 1 timeline: at least 3 interventions, 2 to 3 weeks each

    Weeks 1-3Intervention 1

    Choose a matched Prevent or Teach strategy. Use it every day, log it, and rate the behavior daily. Contact the family for Level 2.

    Weeks 4-6Intervention 2

    Review the data. Working: keep going. Not working: change or add a matched intervention and start a new 2-3 week period.

    Weeks 7-9Intervention 3

    Same review. Keep what helped, change what did not, and keep logging family contact.

    After 6-9 weeksMTSS team

    Three interventions with little or no change: print the Request for Support packet and bring it to the MTSS team.

    Why 2 to 3 weeks each: a behavior needs at least 5 data points in each phase to show a trend (Kratochwill et al., 2013); 10 school days of daily ratings gives a dependable picture (Chafouleas, Riley-Tillman, & Sugai, 2007). Changing sooner hides whether the intervention worked. Why at least 3: the Sunnyside MTSS expectation for documented Tier 1 support. Level 3 and 4 behaviors still go to an administrator right away.
