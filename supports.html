Multilingual Learners & Students with IEPs / 504 Plans

Tier 1 is for every student. These supports layer on top of the Intervention Menu so every Sunnyside student can understand and meet the HEART expectations.

Start here: a student who did not understand a direction is not being defiant. Check language first, then use the matched intervention from the menu.

The plan comes first. IEP and 504 accommodations and any Behavior Intervention Plan are required, not optional. Tier 1 interventions are added to them, never used in place of them.

Request cards

Words to use instead of acting out. Cut apart and keep on the desk or a ring. Sentence frames support English learners (Baker et al., 2014).

Partner talk frames

For structured partner talk (Baker et al., 2014; Sáenz, Fuchs, & Fuchs, 2005).

First / Then board

Visual support (Steinbrenner et al., 2020). Leave blank to write with a dry-erase marker.

Break cards

Teach it first; honor it quickly at first (Kern & Clemens, 2007).

Visual schedule

One step per line. The student checks off each step (Spriggs et al., 2017).
