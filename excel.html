Excel Log Workbook

Create a red-and-black Broncho Excel workbook of the logs saved on this computer: a separate copy you can keep, and a place to keep logging if the website is ever unavailable.

Create your workbook

Stored only where you save it

The workbook is built on this computer. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. To protect student information (FERPA):

  • Save it to your school OneDrive or Teams, not a personal device, USB drive, or personal email.
  • Share it only with staff who work with the student (administrator, counselor, MTSS team).
  • Use initials or student ID, never full names.
  • PowerSchool stays the official record. Delete copies you no longer need.

What's inside

  • All Logs: every entry with date, student, location, Log Type, level, behavior, interventions, response, parent contact, and the full log entry. Discipline rows are highlighted in red. Blank rows have drop-down menus so you can keep logging in Excel.
  • Summary by Student: entries, interventions tried, parent contacts, and whether the Tier 1 timeline is met.
  • Interventions Tried: each intervention with first and last dates and days used.
  • How to Log: the Sunnyside standards, so the workbook works on its own.

Every tab has the Broncho banner, red column headers, and prints landscape with page numbers. It uses the site's fonts (Oswald and Source Sans 3); if they are not installed, Excel shows a similar font. The free fonts are at Google Fonts.

To bring logs back into this website, use the backup file on the Student Logs page. The Excel workbook is a copy for safekeeping and sharing.
